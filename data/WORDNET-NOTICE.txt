Changes made for TỪ TỪ: grouped records by headword, retained individual sense
identifiers and relation targets, extracted synonyms, adjective similarity,
direct antonyms, related forms and explicit inflections, and compressed data
into alphabetical shards. Source version and SHA-256 are in wordnet/source.json.
Separate Vietnamese sense labels in wordnet-vi.json are original editorial
translations for this application. They are not inferred from the bilingual
dictionary's ordering and do not represent official WordNet translations.
